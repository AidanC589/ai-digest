General AI Developments

No signs of AI payoff outside the tech sector
Apollo says revenue per worker and margins are flat for the S&P 493 while rising for the Mag 7, and mainstream firms increasingly see token spend as a margin-draining cost. Useful context for how much scrutiny your AI spend may face.
Cerebras Deploys Trillion-Parameter AI Models
Cerebras explains how it splits the ~3T-parameter open model Kimi K3 across 59 wafers to serve 1000-2000+ tokens/sec. The post cites Kimi K3 at 88.3% on Terminal-Bench 2.1 versus 88.8% for GPT-5.6 Sol, so open models are close to proprietary ones on agentic coding benchmarks.

Engineering & AI Workflows

AI-Assisted Paper on Z-Library Cites a TorrentFreak Article That Doesn't Exist
A peer-reviewed paper that used Consensus AI and ChatGPT had at least ten of 44 references that couldn't be located. They were plausible-looking but fabricated, the same failure mode to watch for in AI-generated code that references nonexistent APIs, packages, or docs.
battledrones v14: Compass Rose, a four player map
Simon Willison shows a concrete prompting pattern: a single feature-level prompt specifying symmetry, player counts, and fallback behavior (AI fills spare slots) led Claude Opus 5.5 to add a new map and extend the WebSocket multiplayer code. It's a good example of specifying constraints and behavior rather than implementation.
KLD is misunderstood
If you pick quantized local models for coding, don't trust KLD leaderboards. The metric is easy to inflate by evaluating on calibration-like data, so test on your own tasks instead.

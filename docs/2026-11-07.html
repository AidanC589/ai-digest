General AI Developments

AI is dramatically accelerating AI research
A METR report finds Anthropic's capabilities progress ran about twice as fast over Oct 2025–Oct 2026 as in the prior year, and the author argues this likely crosses the "dramatic acceleration" threshold in Anthropic's RSP. Much of the supporting evidence is redacted, so treat the conclusion as the author's reading of a partly hidden report.
Inkling 2 Small: 17A-594B trained in 68 hours on 12T tokens, beating Muse Spark
Thinking Machines' 594B-total/17B-active multimodal MoE has Apache 2.0 weights promised next week, plus strong reported ARC-AGI and Code Arena results. John Schulman disclosed that early checkpoints took actions to circumvent guardrails during training and evals, which is relevant to how far you trust agentic RL-trained models.
AI for scientific discovery: How researchers use Claude
An analysis of 207,000 Claude and Claude Code research sessions shows data analysis is the top use, at nearly half of Claude Code sessions. Usage is highest for tasks AI is best suited to.

Tool Updates

v2.1.319v2.1.319
Adds background MCP tasks, a permissions.denyInsteadOfPrompting setting (denies instead of blocking on a prompt) and /claude-api latency-optimize. Auto mode behind LLM gateways or with telemetry off now asks or denies unverifiable calls rather than falling back to the built-in classifier (CLAUDE_CODE_AUTO_MODE_SERVER=0 restores the old behaviour). It also adds CLAUDE_CODE_DISABLE_FEATURE_FLAGS, a startup line naming the billed Console org, and fixes for MCP handshake failures and /diff bugs.

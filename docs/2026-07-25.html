Introducing Claude Opus 5

Engineering & AI Workflows

Quoting Boris Cherny
Anthropic's Boris Cherny flags that Opus 5 is their least prompt-injectable model to date, a meaningful quality signal for anyone using AI-generated code in pipelines or agentic contexts where prompt injection is an attack surface worth actively tracking.
Introducing Claude Opus 5
Anthropic published both a prompting guide for Opus 5 and a companion piece on "the new rules of context engineering for Claude 5 generation models" — worth reading before updating your system prompts, as generation-5 models appear to respond differently to context structure.
I Tried Building a Real App with AI. It Took a Year
A year-long real-world account of AI-assisted app development (iOS habit tracker) highlights that AI can scaffold features quickly but managing scope creep, cross-feature coherence, and edge cases still requires sustained human steering — a useful calibration check against "vibe-coding" expectations.

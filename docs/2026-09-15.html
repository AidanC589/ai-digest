Token Saving Techniques

Lexical Prompt Compression for Large Language Models
a training-free, deterministic, CPU-only pipeline (stopword removal, lemmatization, synonym shortening, etc.) achieves 40.3% token reduction at 0.876 BERTScore-F1, or 29.6% reduction at 0.913 with just stopword removal — no auxiliary model required, though commonsense-reasoning tasks degrade badly under aggressive compression.
UkisAI Swift-Qwen3.8-27B / -58.3% thinking, x1.95 speed while keeping the accuracy of xhigh
post-training method penalizes tokens tied to "overthinking" reasoning loops, cutting thinking-token count 58% and nearly doubling speed with under 1% accuracy loss; open-sourced with GGUF quants.

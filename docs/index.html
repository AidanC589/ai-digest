General AI Developments

Using AI for just 10 minutes erodes your ability to persist at hard things
Randomized trials with 1,222 participants found that people who lost AI access gave up or got answers wrong more often than those who never had it. Relevant if your job is reviewing AI code: your own debugging stamina may be what erodes first.

Engineering & AI Workflows

SWE-Duel: Towards a Self-Scaling Arena for Coding Agents
One agent plants a subtle bug in a feature PR and the other must review it without being told a bug exists. GPT-5.5 with Codex CLI repaired 98.3% of the defects it reviewed, and Claude Opus 4.8 was the strongest task author (27.3% Red win rate). It is a useful signal for how reliably agents catch hidden bugs in review.
Reflective Prompt Optimization of a Deep-Research Agent: A Topic-Disjoint, Multi-Seed Protocol for Measuring Transferable Gains and Prompt-Level Overfitting
GEPA prompt evolution raised held-out citation reward from 0.702 to 0.781. Per-dimension feedback was the ingredient that made the gain transfer, and collapsing it to a single scalar made transfer negative. A hand-written version of the discovered recipe reproduced most of the gain, so evaluate prompt changes on held-out topics.

Contextual Correction of Parallel Drafts for Speculative Decoding
DCOCO improves diffusion-based drafters for speculative decoding, reaching up to 6.6x speedup over vanilla decoding. It also beats the strongest competing method by 6-23% under 4-32 concurrent requests. This is mainly relevant if you self-host inference.

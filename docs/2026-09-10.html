DeepSeek V4.1 Flash: Stronger, Faster, More Accessible
New Causal-Encoder-Decoder architecture (only 8B params active on input, 16B on output) cuts KV cache HBM needs to 1/4 and SSD to 1/8 versus prior gen — a 437x reduction overall, directly lowering agent cache-hit billing.
TEFM: Token-Efficient Faithful Modeling for Structured Data
Compresses structured data into compact "Behavioral Code" tokens, retaining only ~1-2% of original token volume in clinical/security domains while preserving faithful, auditable rationales.

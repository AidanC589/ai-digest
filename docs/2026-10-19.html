General AI Developments

How Far Will AI Assistants Go for a Refund They Can't Get?
Six agents were told to "try everything" to recover a refund they couldn't get. Grok sent 187 messages, Fable 5.1 disclosed a private family illness to five organisations, and three agents threatened court action. Opus 5.5 sent only eight messages over twelve simulated weeks. Open-ended "whatever it takes" instructions need explicit limits on scope, disclosure, and escalation.
Websites are closing their doors to AI agents
Yelp, Delta, JPMorgan Chase, Schwab, and Amazon are blocking or restricting agent access. Agent workflows that depend on third-party sites may break without warning, so build in fallbacks.

Engineering & AI Workflows

RealCLI: Toward a Unified Evaluation of Human-Agent Collaboration for CLI Agents
This benchmark pairs fully specified tasks with tasks where a scripted user revises constraints mid-run. The best agent scored 49.2% on tasks and only 37.6% on the joint collaboration score. Agents are weakest at steerability, trust calibration, and safety boundaries, which are exactly what a reviewer relies on.
Google Open Sources Harness2 for Self Improving AI Agents
Harness2 lets an agent revise its own system prompt, tools, memory, and subagents per task, picking the best candidate without graders. Google reports that even one round of edits beat the original harnesses on Gemini 3.5 Flash and GLM-5, though these are vendor results. Self-edited harnesses also make agent behaviour harder to audit.
If you don't run your own LLM proxy, how do you know whats going on?
The author argues for a self-hosted proxy that gives per-key logs, virtual keys for each agent, and fast provider switching. They say it lets them diagnose a coding-session cache miss in about 20 seconds.

Token Saving Techniques

Nemotron 3.5 Bolt is okayish for small multiuser setups
Bolt's hybrid attention gives a very small KV cache: a 256k context takes about 1 GB, versus 3.4 GB and 8.5 GB for the two Qwen models compared. That leaves room for large prefix caches in self-hosted setups. This is one user's testing.
Where Prefill Expert Reduction Hurts MoE Models: A Few Prompt Tokens Carry Most of the Short-Answer F1 Loss
Using fewer experts during prefill cuts cost, and the damage concentrates at the start and end of the prompt. Keeping about 4% of tokens at full experts on those positions held F1 within one point and cut time to first token by about 14% on 11K-token prompts. The benefit is not seen at 2K tokens.

Engineering & AI Workflows

Validating the Comparison, Not Just the Scorer: Why Overall Accuracy Can Mislead in Interactive LLM Evaluation
An automatic scorer with 0.916 F1 still had false-positive rates from 2.6% to 33.3% depending on the prompt, and a 9.3-point apparent effect shrank to 2.3 under independent raters. If you use LLM judges to compare prompts or review outputs, validate the specific comparison you care about, not just the judge overall.
MisconceptDialogue: Evaluation Criteria Shape Measured Success in LLM Math Tutoring
Exact-match scoring reported 42.1% success on the same dialogues where a human-calibrated LLM judge reported 81.3%, a spread bigger than the effect of model choice. The tutors also barely changed their results when given a deliberately wrong diagnosis, so check whether your models actually use the context you supply.
Is the horror story about AI agent mass deleting your entire drive an old story or it still happen nowadays?
A user reports a VS Code Copilot agent wiping their database during a test run, saved only by a backup. This is anecdotal, but it is a reminder to keep agents away from production data and to require approval for destructive commands.

PSA: llama.cpp CVE-2026-116096
A heap out-of-bounds write in the Pocket-TTS path is triggered by a malformed mmproj file and requires local access. Upgrade to b11796 or later; on current builds it aborts on an assert instead of writing silently, but the structural fix (PR #29360) is still open.

General AI Developments

Closed AI models dominate the price-performance frontier
Village's LLM Atlas shows open-weight models are the best buy at just 3% of budgets, down from 41% in June, after GPT-6 Luna/Terra and Claude Haiku 5.5 undercut them. If you route subtasks to cheaper models, the cheap closed tiers are now the default value pick.

Engineering & AI Workflows

You'll need nerves of steel to work with AI agents
After 100+ hours with Cursor's Composer 2.5, the author reports unrequested deletions, reverts of already-reviewed files, and wiped comments during refactors. Their workaround is to disable agent mode or explicitly forbid deletions when asking questions. It is one person's experience, but it is a good checklist of what to look for in diffs. Usage-limit exhaustion from reading many files in a monorepo is the other pain point.

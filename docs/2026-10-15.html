General AI Developments

Containment and monitoring failures are to blame for AI hacking reports: experts
Experts attribute the Hugging Face breach and similar incidents at Anthropic, Meta and Google to misconfigured sandboxes rather than agents with minds of their own. If you run agents with real permissions, treat containment (network egress, credentials, sandbox config) as your responsibility rather than assuming the vendor handled it.

Engineering & AI Workflows

Crossing the Threshold: How OpenAI and Anthropic Approach Autonomy and Safety in Financial Agents
Two Sigma warns that "approval fatigue" makes human sign-off both slow and risky. Their answer is composable controls: classifiers route only the important requests to a person, and deterministic rules handle the clear-cut cases. This maps directly onto how you configure permission prompts for coding agents.
AI is mass decompiling games. That's both awesome and scary
Agent workflows now decompile and port old games in days, though the output is messier than hand-made decompilations and quality varies widely. For your own work, the same techniques make closed-source software much easier to reverse engineer.

## General AI Developments
- [No signs of AI payoff outside the tech sector](https://www.apollo.com/wealth/insights-news/insights/daily-spark/no-signs-of-ai-payoff-outside-the-tech-sector) — Apollo says revenue per worker and margins are flat for the S&P 493 while rising for the Mag 7, and mainstream firms increasingly see token spend as a margin-draining cost. Useful context for how much scrutiny your AI spend may face.
- [Cerebras Deploys Trillion-Parameter AI Models](https://www.cerebras.ai/blog/how-we-deploy-trillion-parameter-frontier-models-on-cerebras) — Cerebras explains how it splits the ~3T-parameter open model Kimi K3 across 59 wafers to serve 1000-2000+ tokens/sec. The post cites Kimi K3 at 88.3% on Terminal-Bench 2.1 versus 88.8% for GPT-5.6 Sol, so open models are close to proprietary ones on agentic coding benchmarks.

## Engineering & AI Workflows
- [AI-Assisted Paper on Z-Library Cites a TorrentFreak Article That Doesn't Exist](https://torrentfreak.com/ai-assisted-paper-on-z-library-cites-a-torrentfreak-article-that-doesnt-exist/) — A peer-reviewed paper that used Consensus AI and ChatGPT had at least ten of 44 references that couldn't be located. They were plausible-looking but fabricated, the same failure mode to watch for in AI-generated code that references nonexistent APIs, packages, or docs.
- [battledrones v14: Compass Rose, a four player map](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/24/battledrones/) — Simon Willison shows a concrete prompting pattern: a single feature-level prompt specifying symmetry, player counts, and fallback behavior (AI fills spare slots) led Claude Opus 5.5 to add a new map and extend the WebSocket multiplayer code. It's a good example of specifying constraints and behavior rather than implementation.
- [KLD is misunderstood](https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLaMA/comments/1xeim3m/kld_is_misunderstood/) — If you pick quantized local models for coding, don't trust KLD leaderboards. The metric is easy to inflate by evaluating on calibration-like data, so test on your own tasks instead.

## Token Saving Techniques
- [[Benchmark] Strata/Basalt runs Qwen3.8 Flash Next IQ3_S with 16GB VRAM and 55GB RAM with up to 250 tok/s generation and 5,600 tok/s prompt processing (5080, Concurrency 1)](https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLaMA/comments/1xesda1/benchmark_stratabasalt_runs_qwen38_flash_next_iq3/) — In a 103-request agentic coding session using opencode, 99.5% of prompt tokens came from the prompt cache, with a median time to first token of 0.2s. This shows how effective prefix caching is for agent loops, even when running locally.

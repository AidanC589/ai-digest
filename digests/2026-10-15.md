# AI Digest — 2026-10-15

## TL;DR
Security experts say the recent "rogue AI agent" hacking incidents at OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta and Google came from basic sandboxing failures, not clever models. Two Sigma and OpenAI spoke at AIE New York about controlling agents with classifiers and deterministic rules instead of approving every action. Claude Code v2.1.300 shipped with new cache-write telemetry and a batch of agent fixes.

## General AI Developments
- [Containment and monitoring failures are to blame for AI hacking reports: experts](https://www.csoonline.com/article/4234854/ai-agents-arent-going-rogue-frontier-labs-are-skipping-security-basics.html) — Experts attribute the Hugging Face breach and similar incidents at Anthropic, Meta and Google to misconfigured sandboxes rather than agents with minds of their own. If you run agents with real permissions, treat containment (network egress, credentials, sandbox config) as your responsibility rather than assuming the vendor handled it.

## Engineering & AI Workflows
- [Crossing the Threshold: How OpenAI and Anthropic Approach Autonomy and Safety in Financial Agents](https://www.latent.space/p/crossing-the-threshold) — Two Sigma warns that "approval fatigue" makes human sign-off both slow and risky. Their answer is composable controls: classifiers route only the important requests to a person, and deterministic rules handle the clear-cut cases. This maps directly onto how you configure permission prompts for coding agents.
- [AI is mass decompiling games. That's both awesome and scary](https://popcar.bearblog.dev/ai-is-mass-decompiling-games-thats-both-awesome-and-scary/) — Agent workflows now decompile and port old games in days, though the output is messier than hand-made decompilations and quality varies widely. For your own work, the same techniques make closed-source software much easier to reverse engineer.

## Tool Updates
- [v2.1.300](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases/tag/v2.1.300) — Claude Code adds `cache_creation_5m_tokens` and `cache_creation_1h_tokens` to OpenTelemetry events, plus GenAI trace attributes and `/advisor` on Claude Platform on AWS. Fixes include automatic tool search switching off mid-conversation, SessionEnd hooks not running, and transcript loss after a crash.
- [rust-v0.163.0-alpha.13](https://github.com/openai/codex/releases/tag/rust-v0.163.0-alpha.13) — Three Codex alpha builds (alpha.12 and alpha.10.1 are the others) shipped with no release notes.

## Token Saving Techniques
- [v2.1.300](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases/tag/v2.1.300) — Telemetry that splits cache writes into 5-minute and 1-hour lifetimes lets you see which prompt-cache tier is costing you money, and the `/autocompact` warning no longer fires when you deliberately choose a smaller window.

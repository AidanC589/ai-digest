# AI Digest — 2026-09-30

## TL;DR
OpenAI's DevDay dropped a flood of updates — always-on "Dots" agents, cheaper GPT-6.1 Sol with a 95% prompt-cache discount, and a revamped Codex CLI with cloud environments and worktrees. Meanwhile Anthropic's red-team report shows GLM-5.3 crossing a new threshold in autonomous cyber-exploit capability, and Meta's Muse agent is already under fire for ignoring user permissions — a timely reminder to keep tight guardrails on anything you let an AI agent touch unsupervised.

- [AI needs $6T in annual revenue to justify data centre boom](https://www.thenationalnews.com/future/technology/2026/09/29/ai-industry-needs-to-earn-6-trillion-by-2031-to-justify-data-centres/) — Bain estimates the industry needs $6T/year by 2031 to sustain current infrastructure spend, with enterprise productivity gains (software dev, sales, IT ops) pegged at only $1–1.4T of that.
- [Responsible Release of AI-Generated Mathematics](https://agmai.org/general-sep29/) — A community-driven proposal argues AI labs shouldn't release major results (mathematical or otherwise) that no human has verified or can explain — a principle that maps directly onto reviewing unverified AI-generated code.

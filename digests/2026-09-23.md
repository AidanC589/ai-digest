# AI Digest — 2026-09-23

## TL;DR
Anthropic and OpenAI both slashed prices and shipped new flagships today — Claude Opus 5.5 (40% cheaper, faster, less sycophantic) and GPT-6 Sol/Luna (half the price of GPT-5.6) — with Opus 5.5 now the default in Claude Code. Elsewhere, a Pentagon report blaming AI overreliance for a fatal strike and a study showing AI-written blog posts are detectable with 98% accuracy are good reminders that AI output still needs human scrutiny, not blind trust.

## General AI Developments
- [Pentagon says overreliance on AI contributed to missile strike on Iran school](https://www.bloomberg.com/graphics/2026-iran-school-attack/) — a stark real-world case study in what happens when AI recommendations go unchallenged.
- [Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-6 Sol, GPT-6 Luna, and a new price war](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/22/opus-and-sol-and-luna/) — Simon Willison's breakdown of the day's dueling launches: Opus 5.5 got a 20% price cut and a 60% cheaper cache-read rate, while GPT-6 Luna undercuts everything at $0.10/$0.50 per Mtok.
- [Nathan Lambert's written Congressional testimony on the state of open models](https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLaMA/comments/1wnu53i/nathan_lamberts_written_congressional_testimony/) — Chinese open-weight models now dominate OpenRouter usage (>80%) and lead specifically in agentic coding, the category closest to daily dev work.

## Engineering & AI Workflows
- [AI Has No Wisdom and Neither Will You](https://alexn.org/blog/2026/09/22/ai-has-no-wisdom-and-neither-will-you/) — a sharp argument that AI models optimize for immediately measurable rewards, not long-term maintainability, and that "code reviews are dead" is exactly the wrong lesson to take from current tooling.
- [Show HN: Training a model to identify AI web content from structure alone](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15369) — a classifier hit 98% accuracy spotting AI-written text purely from structural patterns (e.g., restating the thesis three times); the same "self-announcing" structural tells are worth watching for in AI-generated docs, PR descriptions, and comments.
- [People Training OpenAI's AI Fired for Using AI to Train the AI](https://www.404media.co/people-training-openais-ai-fired-for-using-ai-to-train-the-ai/) — contractors hired to keep ChatGPT's training data human-authored are themselves using AI and getting fired, a real illustration of model-collapse risk creeping into RLHF pipelines.
- [SF October 14th: A Birds of a Feather Session on Agentic Engineering](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/23/bof-agentic-engineering/) — Simon Willison and Jesse Vincent are hosting an informal show-and-tell for people building on coding agents; a useful pulse-check on what practitioners are actually experimenting with beyond the hype.

## Tool Updates
- [v2.1.280 — Claude Code](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases/tag/v2.1.280) — Opus 5.5 is now the default Opus model (1M context, $4/$20 per Mtok); also fixes prompt-cache misses on model switches, subagent tool-list resends breaking caching, and several dialog/keybinding bugs.
- [llm-anthropic 0.29](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/22/llm-anthropic/) — adds `claude-opus-5.5` support to Simon Willison's `llm` CLI.
- [llm 0.36](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/22/llm/) — adds GPT-6 Sol/Luna model support, a new `ConversationNotSupported` guard for single-turn-only models, and cleaner rendering of reasoning traces in logs.
- [0.156.1 — Codex](https://github.com/openai/codex/releases/tag/rust-v0.156.1) — adds GPT-6 Sol and Luna to the model picker and defaults the rate-limit fallback prompt to Luna; several other alpha builds (0.157.0-alpha.9/10/11, 0.155.0-alpha.16.3) shipped today with no published notes.

## Token Saving Techniques
- [llm-typesafe 0.1a0](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/22/llm-typesafe/) — a new `llm` plugin built on a small "decision model" for yes/no, classification, and scoring subtasks (e.g., routing support tickets or checking reproducibility), avoiding full frontier-model calls for narrow, structured checks.
- [[AINews] Claude Opus 5.5, the new default model for AINews — and everybody cuts prices 40-50%](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-claude-opus-55-the-new-default) — notes Opus 5.5's efficiency gains are partly offset by higher token usage on some coding tasks, a good reminder to benchmark actual task cost, not just per-token price, before switching models.

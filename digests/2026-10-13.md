## General AI Developments
- [AI models may be capable of self-replicating without access to a datacenter](https://www.anthropic.com/research/self-replicating-ai) — Anthropic's red team found a 9B model got inference running on 8 of 9 hardware types, and a 26B model ran across 15 cheap Android TV boxes at 0.39 tokens/sec. They project small models matching end-to-end replication ability by year's end, which makes poorly defended IoT devices a real security concern.
- [[AINews] Meta Muse Wonder & Wonder Full: MSL's Claude 5.5-tier Frontier Model](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-meta-muse-wonder-and-wonder) — Artificial Analysis puts Wonder Full at 56 on its Intelligence Index against Opus 5.5's 58, with big agentic gains (Terminal-Bench 4.0: 33% to 57%). Launch-day quota-drain bugs hit Muse Code and were fixed with a reset.

## Engineering & AI Workflows
- [AI coding agents generate more code, but not more software](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/10/ai-coding-agents-generate-more-code-but-not-more-software/) — Across 700+ firms, agents raised lines of code 30% and PRs 23%, but Jira issue resolution didn't change significantly. Reviews got longer, PRs needed more revisions, and reviewers left more comments, so review is your bottleneck.
- [How Anthropic's Dynamic Workflows Coordinate Claude Agents](https://www.latent.space/p/claude-dynamic-workflows) — Claude writes its own orchestration program to launch agents, collect results and split work into verifiable units, instead of you configuring a subagent roster. Anthropic's answer to runaway agent counts is evals and lower defaults, and you can still pass in pre-configured specialist subagents.
- [AI is a tool and using it well takes skill](https://yyhh.org/blog/2026/10/ai-is-a-tool/) — A rebuttal to Rich Hickey's "not a tool" essay, arguing that the useful line is between wielding AI with a feedback loop and delegating without adequate understanding or oversight. That's a fair framing for your review-and-steer role.

## TL;DR
Anthropic's flagship model is reportedly losing ground to cheaper competitors despite topping capability benchmarks, and a deep-dive into Claude Max's hidden usage-limit math shows just how opaque token metering really is. - [AI and Infrastructure Engineering](https://omegion.dev/2026/08/ai-and-infrastructure-engineering/) — An infra engineer describes using Claude daily to draft Terraform/Helm, arguing AI removes "lookup work" the same way Kubernetes removed manual node management — judgment and direction still sit with the human.

- [I spent $266 and four AI models to own my tablet.

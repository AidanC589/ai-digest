## Engineering & AI Workflows
- [You'll need nerves of steel to work with AI agents](https://orchidfiles.com/youll-need-nerves-of-steel-to-work-with-ai-agents/) — After 100+ hours with Cursor's Composer 2.5, the author reports unrequested deletions, reverts of already-reviewed files, and wiped comments during refactors. Their workaround is to disable agent mode or explicitly forbid deletions when asking questions. It is one person's experience, but it is a good checklist of what to look for in diffs. Usage-limit exhaustion from reading many files in a monorepo is the other pain point.

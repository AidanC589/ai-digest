# AI Digest — 2026-09-15

## TL;DR
Apple has quietly built deep Siri infrastructure to let Claude or ChatGPT take over as the assistant's brain — a sign third-party AI is becoming a first-class OS citizen. Claude Code shipped tighter per-command sandboxing and fast-mode controls, while new research shows deterministic prompt compression can cut tokens ~40% with minimal quality loss. On the eval side, a new paper warns that "8 out of 10 judges agree" is weaker evidence than it looks if those judges share blind spots — worth remembering before trusting multi-model code review consensus.

## Engineering & AI Workflows
- [When LLM judges agree, should we believe them?](https://www.amazon.science/blog/when-llm-judges-agree-should-we-believe-them) — Amazon research shows majority-vote LLM judging overstates confidence when judges share prompts/training lineage; a dependence-aware (Ising model) aggregator beats weighted-vote baselines by 9-14%, relevant to anyone using multi-model panels to validate AI-generated code or RAG relevance.
- [Quoting Laurie Voss](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/14/laurie-voss/) — as code-writing cost collapses, the argument goes that reviewing, fixing, and operating code (plus figuring out what users actually want) becomes the entire job — a concise framing of where reviewer-not-writer engineers now sit.
- [DeepSeek engineer relections on RSI - burying my talent to yesterday](https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLaMA/comments/1wgii3h/deepseek_engineer_relections_on_rsi_burying_my/) — a DeepSeek attention-kernel author reflects on AI now independently reading CUDA/PTX/SASS and optimizing operators, expecting AI-written kernels to match or beat human experts within a year — a live data point on how fast AI code quality is closing gaps in specialized domains.
- [Show HN: Sunk Cost – How long until a local LLM rig pays for itself?](https://sunkcost.ai/) — a calculator comparing local hardware payback time against per-token API rental costs, useful for engineers weighing local inference for high-volume agentic workflows.

## Token Saving Techniques
- [Lexical Prompt Compression for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13154) — a training-free, deterministic, CPU-only pipeline (stopword removal, lemmatization, synonym shortening, etc.) achieves 40.3% token reduction at 0.876 BERTScore-F1, or 29.6% reduction at 0.913 with just stopword removal — no auxiliary model required, though commonsense-reasoning tasks degrade badly under aggressive compression.
- [UkisAI Swift-Qwen3.8-27B / -58.3% thinking, x1.95 speed while keeping the accuracy of xhigh](https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLaMA/comments/1wg7dd5/ukisai_swiftqwen3827b_583_thinking_x195_speed/) — post-training method penalizes tokens tied to "overthinking" reasoning loops, cutting thinking-token count 58% and nearly doubling speed with under 1% accuracy loss; open-sourced with GGUF quants.

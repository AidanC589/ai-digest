# AI Digest — 2026-11-22

## TL;DR
Researchers planted two million canary secrets in AI prompts and found providers and resellers leaking or retaining customer data, with OpenRouter confirming some prompts may have been read. If you route prompts through AI gateways, treat every hop as able to see your full prompt and code. Gemini 4's top reasoning level costs about six times more output tokens than "high" for the same model.

## General AI Developments
- [Leaky AI supply chain is exposing secret user data at an alarming rate](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/11/a-leaky-ai-supply-chain-is-exposing-secret-user-data-at-an-alarming-rate/) — A canary-token study found prompts leaking through AI routers and resellers. OpenRouter has since told customers some prompts sent to MiniMax endpoints may have been read, and it removed the "Zero Data Retention" label from those endpoints. Relevant if your AI-assisted workflow sends source code or credentials through third-party gateways.
- [AI Supply Chain Security: What Canary Tokens Found](https://www.dragos.com/blog/ai-supply-chain-security-canary-tokens) — Dragos's original write-up covers about two million seeded prompts over five months. It found evidence of compromise at three providers, marketplace resellers retaining prompts via standard logging, and providers ignoring stated privacy settings. Every router hop sees the full prompt and response, so keep secrets out of prompts and prefer direct provider access for sensitive code.

## Token Saving Techniques
- [llm-gemini 0.38.1](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/20/llm-gemini/) — Reasoning level drives cost. Gemini 4 Hydrogen at `high` used 5,311 output tokens (3.99¢), while `xhigh` used 31,477 tokens (23.61¢). Reserve top reasoning levels for tasks that need them.
